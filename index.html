A Living Economy of Three Currencies

Not one metric but three, each serving a distinct function that no single token can fulfill.

MYCEL

Soulbound Reputation (0.0 – 1.0)

Non-transferable. Computed from participation, peer recognition, validation quality, and longevity. Decays without activity. Cannot be bought, sold, or gifted — only earned through demonstrated care.

SAP

Circulation Medium (Demurrage-bearing)

2% annual decay returns idle wealth to commons as compost: 70% local, 20% regional, 10% global. Mintable against physical assets. Value that must move or decompose.

TEND

Mutual Credit (Service Hours)

One TEND, one hour. Zero-sum. Credit limits expand counter-cyclically when communities are stressed. Cultural naming is sovereign — TEND, CARE, HOURS — protocol stays interoperable.
